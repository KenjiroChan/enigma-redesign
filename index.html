What people ask us at the front desk

Will it be too scary?

Only the three Rotor II rooms are scary, and none use actors who chase or touch you. The other six aren't scary at all.

Will we be in a room with strangers?

No. Every booking is private, so the only people in your room are the ones you bring.

Can kids play?

Yes. Players aged 10 and under need an adult (18+) in the room with them.

What if we're running late?

Call us as soon as you know. [State exactly how a late arrival affects game time.]

What if our plans change?

Let us know at least 48 hours before your booking and we'll move you to another time or give you store credit for a future visit.
